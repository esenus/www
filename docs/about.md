# About esenus

**esenus — Connect with information.**

esenus is an ongoing practice of making, experimenting, using, recording, and preserving what comes from these activities.

Experiments, prototypes, failures, work in progress, thoughts, things made or used, and past records are all part of the activity.

What is created or discovered in one activity can connect to another activity, idea, or record.

esenus does not require every activity to become a finished product. Activities may remain experiments, develop into ongoing Projects, or grow into Products over time.

## Principles

- **Clear** — Keep structures understandable.
- **Flexible** — Allow activities and systems to change.
- **Playful** — Leave room for exploration and experimentation.
- **Quiet** — Avoid unnecessary explanation and functionality.
- **Imperfect** — Treat unfinished work and failures as part of the process.
- **Minimal** — Build only what is needed.



## Projects

esenus consists of independent Projects with different roles.

Loom provides a way to create and publish websites. Muse presents activities and the things that emerge from them. Nest provides personal systems for organizing and using information.

These Projects are not parts of a single application. They remain independent while connecting through the information and activities they produce.

## Scope

esenus does not prescribe a fixed scale, format, or destination for its activities.

A small experiment, an unfinished idea, a personal system, or a developed Product can all exist within esenus.

The emphasis is on the activities themselves and the connections that emerge from them.
